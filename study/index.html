02 / STUDY

Study at a German university.

Studying in Germany starts with checking whether your previous qualification gives you access to higher education and then finding programs whose admission requirements you meet.

Requirements

Check the academic requirements first.

Admission depends on the qualification you hold, the university and the specific degree program. There is no single requirement for every applicant.

01

University entrance qualification

Your previous education must provide the required access to higher education in Germany. The exact assessment depends on your qualification and country.

02

Degree program

Admission requirements can differ by program, including subject background, grades, prerequisites and other program-specific criteria.

03

Language

The required language depends on the language of instruction and the university program. German-taught programs commonly require proof of German.

04

Application route

Applications may be submitted directly to the university or through an external application service such as uni-assist, depending on the university and program.

05

Documents

Typical documents can include certificates, transcripts, language certificates and other materials specified by the university.

06

Financing

International students may need to demonstrate sufficient financial resources, especially when applying for a student visa.

Typical sequence

From qualification to enrolment.

01

04

After admission, arrange the remaining steps, including financing, visa and enrolment where applicable.
