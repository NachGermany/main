Professional routes

Your occupation changes the requirements.

Germany distinguishes between different professions and employment routes. Recognition is especially important for regulated professions.

01

Qualification

Your vocational or academic qualification is one of the main factors in determining which employment routes may be available.

02

Professional experience

Relevant professional experience can be important for certain employment and residence routes.

03

Recognition

Recognition is mandatory for certain regulated professions. For non-regulated professions, different rules can apply.

04

German language

Language requirements depend on the occupation, employer and residence route. Regulated professions can have additional professional language rules.

05

Employment

Many professional residence routes are connected to qualified employment and therefore depend on the actual job and employment conditions.

06

Residence route

The appropriate visa or residence permit depends on the applicant's nationality, qualification, employment and individual circumstances.
