COMMON QUESTIONS

Before you choose.

Do these packages guarantee admission or a visa?

No. Universities and public authorities make their own decisions. NachGermany helps you prepare a stronger, more organised application.

Are external fees included?

No. You pay university, uni-assist, APS, translation, notarisation, insurance, courier and government fees directly to the relevant provider.

Can I begin with free tools?

Yes. Our tools and route guides are free. Create an account when you want to save progress, or choose support only when it is useful to you.

Not sure which level of support fits?

Start with a free profile conversation. We’ll help you understand the route before you choose a package.
