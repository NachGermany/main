Study at a German university.

Studying in Germany starts with checking whether your previous qualification gives you access to higher education and then finding programs whose admission requirements you meet.

Requirements

Check your academic eligibility first.

Admission depends on your previous qualification, the university and the specific degree program. There is no single admission requirement for every international student.

01

University entrance qualification

Your previous education must provide the required access to higher education in Germany. The assessment depends on your qualification and country.

02

Degree program

Programs can have specific requirements involving subjects, grades, prerequisites or previous study.

03

Language

The required language depends on the program's language of instruction and the university's admission requirements.

04

Application route

Applications may be submitted directly to the university or through a platform such as uni-assist, depending on the program.

05

Documents

Typical documents can include certificates, transcripts, language certificates and other materials specified by the university.

06

Financing

Applicants may need to demonstrate sufficient financial resources, particularly when applying for a student visa.

Typical sequence

From qualification to enrolment.

01

Check your qualification

04

Admission and next steps

After admission, arrange the remaining steps, including financing, visa and enrolment where applicable.
