01 / AUSBILDUNG

Vocational training in Germany.

Ausbildung is a vocational route that can combine practical training with vocational education. Requirements depend on the occupation and the type of Ausbildung.

Requirements

There is no single Ausbildung requirement.

Requirements vary according to the occupation and whether the training is dual or school-based. The training company or institution can also set its own requirements.

01

School qualification

For dual Ausbildung, German law does not generally require one specific school-leaving qualification, although companies often set their own criteria. School-based Ausbildung can have different rules.

02

German language

German is normally important because training, vocational school and examinations are conducted in German. B1 is commonly relevant, but the exact requirement depends on the route.

03

Training position

For a normal Ausbildung route, you need to identify and apply for a suitable training position with the relevant company or institution.

04

Application

Applications normally include documents such as a CV, certificates and other materials requested by the training provider.

05

Recognition

Whether a school qualification or other document needs formal recognition depends on the type of Ausbildung and the specific situation.

06

Visa / residence

Applicants from outside the EU generally need the appropriate visa or residence permission. Additional requirements can include language and proof of sufficient financial means.
